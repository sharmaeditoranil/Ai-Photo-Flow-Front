📊 Where Does the Data Go & How to Connect to Google Sheets?

1. Instant Local Storage: Every time a user fills the Sign In / Sign Up form or downloads the application, their lead record is saved in high-speed local browser storage (`photoFlowLeads`). It never gets lost and you can export all leads with 1 click to Excel/CSV anytime.

2. Direct WhatsApp Follow-up: In the table above, clicking the green WhatsApp button next to any lead immediately opens WhatsApp with their phone number pre-filled so you or your Quick Art Academy team can call or message them.

3. Cloud Webhook / Google Sheets: To forward registrations straight to your live Google Sheet or CRM in real-time, you can connect a free webhook URL (Google Apps Script / Make.com / Zapier) in js/main.js under saveLeadRecord().

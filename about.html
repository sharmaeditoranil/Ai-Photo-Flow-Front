The Creation Story

The Problem With Western AI on Indian Weddings

The Indian Wedding Reality: A single 3-day Indian wedding shoots between 3,000 and 12,000 RAW files across multiple camera bodies. Photographers spend 25 to 30 sleepless hours after every shoot just doing manual sorting: checking which bride/groom eyes blinked, which burst frame is sharpest, and correcting awful yellow halogen stage lighting.

"Foreign software from the US or Europe is trained on pastel western daylight portraits. When you import Indian wedding photos with dark crimson bridal lehengas, gold zardozi jewelry, and warm mandap fire, their algorithms oversaturate reds into blown-out neon blobs and turn our clients' skin tones burnt orange. On top of that, they force you to upload 150 GB of RAW files over slow studio internet."

Our Engineering Breakthrough: In 2024, Anil Sharma partnered with machine learning engineers to build Ai PhotoFlow:

  • ✓ 100% Offline & Local: LibRaw C-bindings decode RAW files in 11ms on your local GPU/CPU. Zero cloud dependencies.
  • ✓ YCrCb Skin Protection: Isolates natural human skin tones before any color curve is applied.
  • ✓ Universal +5% Vibrance Boost: Calibrated specifically to make jewelry and fabrics pop while keeping faces creamy.
  • ✓ 14-Day Free Trial: Full software access to test on your own live wedding shoots.

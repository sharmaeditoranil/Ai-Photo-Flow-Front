Our Story

Don't just learn the software. Learn to feel the story.

"Most editing courses only teach software shortcuts. They tell you which button to press, but never why to make a cut, how to hold an emotional frame, or how to work with high-value wedding clients. At Quick Art, students step into a real production studio from day one."

Anil started in the editing suite more than ten years ago. After directing and editing hundreds of cinematic wedding films, commercials and creative campaigns, he saw a clear gap in conventional training: students learned tools, not craft.

Under his one-on-one mentorship, students cut broadcast-grade wedding teasers, master DaVinci Resolve colour grading, design rich soundscapes, and build the confidence to run profitable studios or work as senior post-production artists.

That same frontline studio experience led to Ai PhotoFlow — an AI photo editor, AI image editor and photo correction app that removes 25+ hours of manual culling and protects authentic Indian skin tones, running entirely on your own PC or Mac.

Why Ai PhotoFlow Exists

The Problem With Western AI on Indian Weddings

A single three-day Indian wedding produces 3,000 to 12,000 RAW files across multiple camera bodies. Photographers spend 25–30 hours after every shoot on manual sorting — spotting blinks, picking the sharpest burst frame, and correcting harsh yellow stage lighting.

In 2024, Anil Sharma partnered with machine-learning engineers to build a tool designed for this reality.

"Software built in the US or Europe is trained on pastel daylight portraits. With deep crimson lehengas, gold zardozi and warm mandap light, it pushes reds to neon and turns skin orange — and it expects you to upload 150 GB of RAW files over studio internet."

100% Offline & Local

LibRaw C-bindings decode RAW files in 11 ms on your own GPU/CPU. No cloud uploads, ever.

YCrCb Skin Protection

Isolates natural skin tones before any colour curve is applied, so faces stay true.

Calibrated +5% Vibrance

Tuned so jewellery and fabrics come alive while skin stays soft and natural.

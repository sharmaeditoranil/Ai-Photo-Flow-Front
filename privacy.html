Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026 · Effective Immediately

1. Core Privacy Principle: 100% Offline-First

At Ai PhotoFlow (a product of Quick Art Photography & Academy, founded by Anil Sharma), we respect the privacy of wedding photographers, commercial studios, and their clients.

Ai PhotoFlow is engineered as a native desktop application (macOS and Windows). Your original camera RAW files, high-resolution JPEGs, project catalogs, and edits NEVER leave your computer. All artificial intelligence models, facial focus scoring, skin retouching algorithms, and LibRaw debayering execute locally on your physical CPU and GPU. We operate zero cloud photo processing servers.

2. Client Proofing Galleries & 7-Day Auto-Purge

When you voluntarily use the Client Link proofing feature to share a gallery with the bride and groom:

  • Only lightweight, watermarked, compressed preview proxies are uploaded to our dedicated album server (https://license.aiphotoflow.in/album/).
  • Original high-resolution RAW files remain untouched on your local computer.
  • Automatic 7-Day Purge: Uploaded preview images are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers 7 days after the client submits their selections. The selections remain stored inside your local desktop software.

3. Account & Licensing Information We Collect

To manage cryptographic licenses and provide technical customer support, we collect minimal operational information when you create an account, purchase a license, or apply as a partner:

  • Contact Information: Full Name, Studio Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp Mobile Number.
  • Machine Hardware ID: A non-reversible cryptographic SHA-256 hash of your computer's motherboard/CPU ID, used solely to bind your offline Ed25519 commercial license seats.
  • Billing Data: Transaction references, plan type, and purchase date.

4. Payment Processing Security

All digital payment transactions are processed securely through Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway). Ai PhotoFlow never accesses, records, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV codes, or net banking passwords.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Marketing

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or studio information to any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or data brokers.

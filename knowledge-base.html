1. Quickstart & Installation Guide

Ai PhotoFlow is engineered as a standalone native desktop application with bundled local Python multiprocessing runtimes, OpenCV, and LibRaw binaries. No external Python installation is required.

macOS Setup (Apple Silicon M1 / M2 / M3 / M4)

1. Download Ai PhotoFlow-1.0.0-arm64.dmg.
2. Double-click the DMG and drag Ai PhotoFlow into your /Applications folder.
3. On first launch, if prompted by macOS Gatekeeper: Right-click > Open, or allow in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
4. Launch the app and grant Read/Write disk permissions to your external editing SSDs.

Windows Setup (Windows 10 / 11 64-bit)

1. Download Ai PhotoFlow Setup 1.0.0.exe.
2. Run the installer and choose your installation directory.
3. If Windows SmartScreen displays "Windows protected your PC", click More Info > Run Anyway.
4. Launch Ai PhotoFlow from the desktop shortcut.

2. RAW Camera Ingestion & Tiered Previews

Ai PhotoFlow reads high-bitdepth RAW sensor data non-destructively. Your original camera files are NEVER modified, overwritten, or moved.

Supported Camera Formats

• Sony: .ARW, .SR2 (A7IV, A7RV, A1, A9III, FX3, A7SIII)
• Canon: .CR2, .CR3 (EOS R5, R6 Mark II, R3, 5D Mark IV, 1DX)
• Nikon: .NEF, .NRW (Z8, Z9, Z6III, D850)
• Fujifilm: .RAF (X-T5, X-H2S, GFX 100II)
• Adobe: .DNG (Leica, Drone, and converted files)
• Standard: .JPG, .JPEG, .PNG, .TIFF, .WEBP

Two-Tier Preview Pipeline

1. Tier 1 (EXIF Buffer Extract): Sub-15ms generation for instant 10,000 photo grid scrolling.
2. Tier 2 (Linear Proxy Cache): Half-sized linear proxies stored in .photoflow_cache/ for 100% pixel loupe inspection.

3. AI Culling & Micro-Expression Detection

The culling engine evaluates each frame through a 4-tier computer vision pipeline: 

[RAW / JPG Image]
       │
       ▼
 [1. Sharpness & Motion Blur Analysis (Laplacian Variance + High-Freq Gradient)]
       │
       ▼
 [2. Facial Recognition & Micro-Expression (Haar + Dlib Landmarks)]
       │  ├── Eyes Open / Closed Ratio
       │  └── Blinking & Squint Detection
       ▼
 [3. Dynamic Range & Exposure Clipping]
       │  ├── Highlight Burn Check (Bridal Jewelry / Silk Whites)
       │  └── Shadow Crush Check (Groom Tuxedo / Sherwani Blacks)
       ▼
 [Composite Quality Score: 0.0 - 100.0] ──> BEST / PICK / REVIEW / REJECT

Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Thresholds

• EAR ≥ 0.25: Eyes Wide Open (High confidence)
• 0.18 ≤ EAR < 0.25: Micro-squint or candid emotional moment (Review)
• EAR < 0.18: Closed Eyes / Flash Blinking (Auto-downgraded to Reject)

4. Duplicate Burst Clustering & Winner Selector

When photographers capture rapid sequences of 3 to 10 frames, Ai PhotoFlow clusters them using a 3-layer perceptual similarity algorithm:

1. Temporal Delta: Δt ≤ 3.5 seconds based on camera EXIF timestamp.
2. Structural Gradients: 64-bit dHash and pHash difference vectors.
3. Color Consistency: Normalized HSV histogram intersection (Threshold T ≥ 0.90).

Within each burst cluster, the algorithm compares facial sharpness, smile confidence, and open eyes to select the Rank 1 Winner. In the AI Best view, only the winner appears, eliminating 70% of visual clutter.

5. AI Color Engine & +5% Vibrance Boost

Engineered by Anil Sharma specifically to overcome the challenges of Indian wedding lighting:

YCrCb Chrominance Isolation

Human skin pixels fall within the distinct chrominance coordinates: Cr ∈ [133, 173] and Cb ∈ [77, 127]. Ai PhotoFlow constructs a real-time mask around these pixels, locking original complexions before auto-color curves are applied.

Universal +5% Vibrance Boost

Vibrance_applied = clip(Vibrance_calculated + 5.0, 5.0, 35.0)

Boosts undersaturated background hues (mandap flowers, stage lighting, gold ornaments) by an extra 5% while preventing skin from turning orange or red.

9. Troubleshooting Common Issues

1. "File Format Not Recognized"

Verify that your camera uses standard uncompressed or lossless compressed RAW. High-efficiency Sony Lossless and Canon CR3 formats are fully supported in v1.0.0.

2. "Insufficient GPU Memory for Batch Export"

If your machine has less than 8GB of VRAM, navigate to Settings > Performance and set Concurrent Worker Threads to 2 instead of Auto.

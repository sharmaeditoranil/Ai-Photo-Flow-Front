1. Quickstart & Installation Guide

Ai PhotoFlow is a high-performance native desktop application engineered with bundled local Python multiprocessing runtimes, LibRaw 16-bit decoding, and OpenCV. It operates 100% locally on your machine's hardware with no external environment setup needed.

macOS Setup (Apple Silicon M1 / M2 / M3 / M4)

1. Download Ai PhotoFlow-1.0.0-arm64.dmg.
2. Double-click the DMG and drag Ai PhotoFlow into your /Applications folder.
3. On first launch, if prompted by macOS Gatekeeper: Right-click > Open, or click "Open Anyway" in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
4. Launch the app and grant Full Disk Access to your external editing SSDs.
5. Starts automatically with a 1-Day Unrestricted Free Trial (1 machine per customer).

Windows Setup (Windows 10 / 11 64-bit)

1. Download Ai PhotoFlow Setup 1.0.0.exe.
2. Run the installer and choose your installation path (bundled with embedded Python 3.10).
3. If Windows SmartScreen displays "Windows protected your PC", click More Info > Run Anyway.
4. Launch Ai PhotoFlow from the desktop shortcut.

3. 16-Bit RAW Engine & Format Support

Equipped with native LibRaw debayering, Ai PhotoFlow reads 14-bit and 16-bit camera sensor data without compression artifacts or cloud conversion delay.

Supported Camera Formats

4. AI Culling Categories & Micro-Expression Detection

Unlike generic photo managers that score on a single blurry slider, Ai PhotoFlow separates photos into 5 actionable studio categories:

1. AI Best

Every sharp, high-scoring photo plus the single top winner of each burst cluster.

2. Review

Borderline focus, ambiguous emotional expressions, or extreme backlight contrast requiring human judgment.

3. Rejected

Clearly unusable frames: camera shake, subject motion blur, misfires, or black/white test shots.

4. Similar Groups

Burst alternates and near-duplicate frames. The winner is shown in AI Best, alternates kept here.

5. Customer Selected

Photos marked as favorites by clients in their online proofing album.

Album-Size Face Focus Metric

Sharpness is computed on full-resolution files at album scale (256px re-blur metric, independent of skin texture). In group portraits, focus is evaluated on the 2nd-best face so one lucky individual cannot pass a shaken family group.

Candid Emotion Protection: Closed eyes during intense candid moments (tears during Vidai, devotional prayer, joyful bridal laughter) are recognized by our facial expression engine and maintained in AI Best rather than dropped to Rejected.

5. Auto Edit Pipeline & "Pure Light" Color Science

The Auto Edit engine (default style "Pure Light") executes a calibrated closed-loop adjustment pipeline designed specifically for mixed Indian banquet lighting:

1. AI Skin Retouch: Heals blemishes, smooths harsh textures, and applies mattifier strictly on camera pixels gated by a per-person skin mask (eyes, lips, jewellery, and hair remain razor-sharp).
2. AI White Balance: Grey-pixel, grey-edge, and white-patch multi-pass calibration.
3. Clean Colour Closed-Loop: Neutral whites and greys finish slightly cool (b* ≈ -1.5, "halka blue"), eliminating the dirty yellow cast of tungsten banquet halogens while locking skin hue ≤ 50°.
4. Subject Light Balancing: Flash-burned faces are gently lowered to ~160 neutral brightness without dulling background depth.
5. Auto Straighten: Detects dominant architecture verticals and stage horizontals, applying max 3° correction with crop-to-fill (no mirrored borders).

6. Skin Colour Lock & Per-Face Fill Light

Two breakthrough innovations engineered by Anil Sharma solve the biggest headaches in Indian wedding editing:

Skin Colour Lock

When cold white balance is applied to fix yellow banquets, skin complexions often turn grey, ashy, or lifeless. Ai PhotoFlow’s Skin Colour Lock isolates human skin tones (hue 34°–50°, chroma ×1.04) and restores rich, natural Indian complexions after the background has been cooled.

Skin Glow & Per-Face Fill Light

The Skin Glow slider (default 65 "Natural") delivers soft micro-radiance on skin highlights (+2 avg / +5 specular) combined with an intelligent fill light engine that operates only on faces cast in shade (up to +5 L*), while perfectly exposed faces remain untouched.

This indicates that your 1-Day Trial has expired or your machine license token has not been activated. Open Pricing in the app, confirm your active plan badge, and click Retry Upload.

On Windows, Ai PhotoFlow loads the YuNet neural face model directly from RAM to prevent path encoding issues with special characters. Open C:\Users\<username>\.photoflow\backend.log and verify that "YuNet face model loaded" is logged.
